# VengeStats

**VengeStats was created to document and notify people on how players perform against former teams, highlighting the revenge narrative in sports.** Our goal is to provide insights into how players fare in these matchups and expand to multiple sports leagues over time.

---

## **📢 Twitter Bot**

Follow the bot on [X (formerly Twitter)](https://x.com/VengeStats) for daily updates on revenge games. The bot automatically posts when a player faces their former team, and future updates will enhance the tweets with more detailed stats and visuals.

### **🚀 Upcoming Features:**

- **Statistical Insights in Tweets**: Show career averages vs. former teams.
- **Image Generation (like StatMuse)**: Auto-generate graphics with player stats.
- **Multi-League Support**: Expanding beyond the NBA to NFL, MLB, and NHL.

---

## **🛠️ Backend Development**

We're building an API and automation tools to enhance VengeStats' capabilities.

### **🔧 Work in Progress:**

- **Public API**: Query revenge games and player stats.
- **Automated Roster Updates**: Keep track of player trades and signings.
- **Scheduling Optimization**: Ensure timely updates based on game schedules.

---

## **🎨 Frontend Development**

The frontend will provide an interactive experience for users to explore revenge games and analyze player performances.

### **🔧 Planned Features:**

- **Daily Revenge Games Dashboard**: Display all revenge games for the current day.
- **Search & Filters**: Look up players, teams, and past revenge game performances.
- **Stat Visualizations**: Show player performance trends against former teams.
- **Mobile-Friendly UI**: Ensure accessibility across devices.
